## 1–4. AIMS scope, context, and stakeholders

### AIMS scope statement

The LCS AIMS governs the responsible design, development, procurement, provision, operation, monitoring, and retirement of AI systems used in LCS products or internal operations. It applies to Resolve, Atlas, Pulse, their datasets, model providers, deployment environments, employees operating them, and material third parties.

### Context and interested parties

| Context issue | AIMS response |
|---|---|
| Rapid model/vendor changes | Version inventory, change review, vendor notifications, release gates. |
| Customer trust and contractual obligations | Clear system information, incident process, quality monitoring, human escalation. |
| Privacy, security, and regional requirements | Data classification, access control, privacy/security review, region-aware deployment. |
| Workforce adoption and automation bias | Training, role clarity, human oversight, feedback channels. |
| Growth of AI use cases | Central inventory, triage, and governance-board approval. |

## 5. Leadership

### AI policy

LCS will use AI only where it advances legitimate business objectives while protecting people, customer trust, security, privacy, fairness, accessibility, and human accountability. AI outputs are not treated as authoritative when they can materially affect a person, customer relationship, or business decision; qualified people retain responsibility and may override or stop the system.

### Governance roles

| Role | Accountability |
|---|---|
| Board / executive sponsor | Approves policy, objectives, risk appetite, and material risk acceptance. |
| AI governance board | Approves sensitive systems, reviews AIIAs, material changes, incidents, metrics, and corrective actions. |
| AI risk lead | Maintains risk methodology, inventory, AIIA process, SoA, and audit evidence. |
| Product owner | Defines intended use, achieves operational controls, owns monitoring and change requests. |
| Engineering lead | Implements secure and testable system design, version controls, rollback, and technical monitoring. |
| Security and privacy leads | Review data use, vendor commitments, access, incidents, and applicable obligations. |
| Operations owner | Ensures trained use, human oversight, quality checks, and user feedback. |

## 6. Planning

### Risk criteria

Risk score = impact severity × likelihood. Scores 1–5 are low, 6–11 moderate, 12–15 high, and 16–25 critical. High risks require documented treatment and governance-board approval; critical risks require redesign, executive acceptance, or non-deployment.

### AI risk register

| Risk | Systems | Initial score | Treatment | Residual target | Owner |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| Hallucinated/incorrect content | Resolve, Atlas | 16 | Retrieval grounding, human review where customer-facing, evaluations, citations, fallback. | 12 | Product owners |
| Sensitive-data exposure | Resolve, Atlas, Pulse | 15 | Data classification, minimization, vendor terms, encryption, RBAC, logging controls. | 8 | Privacy + security |
| Vendor/model behavior change | Resolve, Atlas | 12 | Version inventory, change notice, test gate, rollback and manual fallback. | 6 | Engineering |
| Automation bias | Resolve, Pulse | 12 | Human decision authority, training, sampled review, explanation/confidence information. | 8 | Operations |
| Unequal performance | Resolve | 12 | Language-based quality monitoring and remediation threshold. | 9 | Support QA |
| Forecast misuse for people decisions | Pulse | 15 | Explicit prohibition, access restriction, governance approval for any new purpose. | 5 | Finance owner |

## 8. Operation

### Operational lifecycle gates

| Gate | Minimum evidence | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| Idea | Use-case card; initial triage; intended/prohibited uses | Product owner |
| Build | Data/model/vendor details; security/privacy design | Engineering, security, privacy |
| Validate | Quality and safety tests; accessibility/language tests where relevant | QA + product owner |
| Deploy | AIIA, risk treatment, rollback, user communication, support plan | Governance board for sensitive systems |
| Operate | Monitoring, feedback, incidents, periodic review | Service owner |
| Change / retire | Material-change assessment or retirement plan | Governance board / product owner |

## 10. Improvement

| Event | Required response |
|---|---|
| Suspected serious misinformation, privacy, security, or discrimination event | Immediately disable or restrict affected feature; initiate incident response; notify governance board. |
| Nonconformity | Record issue, investigate cause, define corrective action, assign owner/due date, verify effectiveness. |
| Material system change | Rerun relevant test, risk, and impact-assessment activities before release. |
| Audit finding or user feedback trend | Add improvement to backlog; review at next management review. |
