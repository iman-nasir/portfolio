## Outcome by day 90

LCS has an operating minimum viable AIMS for the three systems, one approved AI impact assessment, a live inventory and risk register, and executive oversight evidence.

| Time | Work | Deliverable | Exit criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1–15 | Establish governance foundation | Executive sponsor, governance board, AIMS scope, AI inventory, controlled glossary | Every known AI system has a named product and risk owner. |
| Days 16–30 | Classify and map systems | Use-case cards, lifecycle maps, ecosystem/vendor maps, data inventory | Resolve, Atlas, and Pulse have intended/prohibited-use statements. |
| Days 31–45 | Assess risks and impacts | Risk methodology/register; complete Resolve AIIA; triage Atlas and Pulse | No sensitive system operates without approved assessment conditions. |
| Days 46–60 | Implement operational controls | Release gates, human-oversight rules, vendor review, documentation repository, training | Agents cannot auto-send Resolve outputs; Atlas index restrictions enforced. |
| Days 61–75 | Monitor and assure | KPI dashboard, QA sampling, incident/CAPA workflow, internal-audit checklist | First monthly monitoring pack produced. |
| Days 76–90 | Review and improve | Management-review meeting, corrective-action plan, next-quarter backlog | Executives approve objectives, risks, resourcing, and improvements. |

## Immediate decisions needed for a real implementation

| Decision | Recommended default |
|---|---|
| AIMS scope | Start with all customer-facing and internally material AI systems; expand quarterly. |
| Human oversight | Prohibit autonomous external customer communication and adverse individual decisions. |
| Vendor data terms | No training on LCS/customer content; documented retention, location, security, and change commitments. |
| AIIA threshold | Full assessment for customer-facing, personal-data, sensitive, or material-impact systems. |
| Governance cadence | Monthly operational review; quarterly executive management review. |

## Portfolio review checklist

- Is every in-scope AI system inventoried and assigned an owner?
- Does every system have intended and prohibited uses?
- Are current AI risks and impact assessments documented?
- Are human oversight, vendor, data, lifecycle, and monitoring controls functioning?
- Are management review, audit, incidents, and corrective actions evidenced?
- Are material changes blocked until reassessment is complete?
