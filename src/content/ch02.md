## AIIA operating process

| Step | Result |
|---|---|
| Trigger | New AI system, a material model/data/prompt/deployment change, serious incident, or new use case. |
| Triage | Classify system as standard, sensitive, or restricted. Restricted uses are not deployed without executive and legal approval. |
| Scope | Name the system/version, intended users, affected people, deployment context, and foreseeable misuse. |
| Evidence collection | Gather system, data, model, vendor, testing, deployment, stakeholder, and prior-incident information. |
| Impact analysis | Identify benefits and harms; assess severity, likelihood, affected scale, reversibility, and control effectiveness. |
| Treatment | Avoid, reduce, transfer/share, accept with approval, or retire the use case. |
| Approval | Product owner, AI risk lead, security/privacy representatives, and governance board approve proportionate to impact level. |
| Monitor and review | Monitor metrics and events; reassess after a trigger or at least annually. |

## Triage thresholds

| Category | Criteria | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Drafting, search, or forecasting with human review and no sensitive data or adverse decisions. | Streamlined AIIA; product-owner approval. |
| Sensitive | Customer-facing output, personal data, material financial/customer effect, or broad user base. | Full AIIA; governance-board approval. |
| Restricted | Biometric identification, emotion recognition, autonomous adverse decisions, unreviewed high-stakes use, or prohibited data use. | Do not deploy unless exceptional executive/legal approval establishes legality and controls. |

## Completed AI system impact assessment: Resolve v1.0

### 6.2 Scope

Resolve drafts customer-support answers for enterprise software users in English, French, and German. It is used by LCS support agents in North America and Europe. The assessed impact includes customers who receive replies, support agents who rely on drafts, and customers whose support experience may be affected by inconsistent or unsafe information.

### 6.3 AI system information

| Field | Result |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Reduce support-agent research time while preserving agent decision authority. |
| Intended use | Draft responses grounded in approved product documentation and open tickets. |
| Capabilities | Summarization, retrieval, drafting, citation, language translation. |
| Limitations | May hallucinate, misunderstand context, omit caveats, or give uneven quality by language. |
| Foreseeable misuse | Agent sends draft without review; use for account changes or regulated advice; prompts contain unnecessary personal data. |

### 6.4 Data information and quality

| Data category | Control and finding |
|---|---|
| Knowledge base | Only published/approved product documentation; source owner and last-review date are required. |
| Support ticket content | Redacted where feasible; no use for model training; retention follows existing support policy. |
| Quality | Retrieval coverage and stale-content checks are performed monthly. Unsupported answers must display a caution. |

### 6.5 Algorithm and model information

Resolve uses a third-party hosted large language model with retrieval augmentation. LCS records model provider, model version, prompt version, retrieval index version, evaluation set, service region, and vendor change notices. Model changes require a material-change review.

### 6.6 Deployment environment

Resolve is available only in the authenticated agent console. It uses role-based access; agents may not expose model prompts or internal citations to unauthorized users. Every draft and final send is logged under LCS retention policy. There is a manual fallback to ordinary support workflows.

### 6.7 Interested parties

| Party | Potential concern | Engagement method |
|---|---|---|
| Customers | Incorrect, misleading, inaccessible, or privacy-invasive responses. | Feedback link, complaint process, escalation to human support. |
| Support agents | Over-reliance, workload changes, unclear accountability. | Training, quality sampling, user feedback sessions. |
| LCS | Customer trust, legal/contractual exposure, operational reliability. | Governance review, incident reporting, KPIs. |
| Vendor | Data processing, availability, model-version change. | Contract controls, vendor review, service monitoring. |

### 6.8 Impacts: benefits and harms

Impact score = severity (1–5) × likelihood (1–5), after existing controls. Scores of 12+ require board approval; scores of 16+ require redesign or non-deployment.

| Impact | People affected | Benefit / harm | Score | Treatment and residual decision |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Faster, more consistent product help | Customers and agents | Benefit | n/a | Maintain retrieval-quality and response-time monitoring. |
| Fabricated or incorrect answer | Customers | Harm | 12 | Mandatory human review, citations, confidence cue, complaint escalation, quality sampling. Accepted only with monthly review. |
| Uneven quality across supported languages | Non-English customers | Harm | 9 | Language-specific evaluation set, monitor escalation and satisfaction rates by language. |
| Disclosure of personal or confidential information | Customers and LCS | Harm | 10 | Input minimization, access control, no-training contract, log redaction, privacy incident route. |
| Automation bias by support agents | Support agents and customers | Harm | 12 | Training, agent-attestation, visible “draft only” label, quality audits, no auto-send. |
| Service outage or unsafe vendor model change | Customers and agents | Harm | 8 | Version pinning where available, vendor notices, rollback and manual fallback. |

### 6.9 Measures, decision, and monitoring

**Approval decision:** approved as a sensitive system, subject to the following release conditions:

- Human review and manual send are technically enforced.
- Drafts contain source citations and an “AI-generated draft” cue for agents.
- No ticket/customer content is used to train the vendor model.
- Language-quality tests pass defined thresholds before expanding language coverage.
- A severe misinformation or data incident triggers immediate disablement, incident response, and reassessment.

| Metric | Threshold | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Unsupported-answer rate in sample | < 3% monthly | Support QA lead |
| Agent acceptance without substantive edit | Monitor for abrupt increase; investigate automation bias | Support operations lead |
| Escalation / complaint rate by language | No material disparity unexplained by case mix | Product owner |
| Privacy/security incidents | 0 severe incidents; immediate escalation for any suspected event | Security and privacy leads |
| Knowledge-base stale-content rate | < 2% in active retrieval corpus | Documentation owner |
