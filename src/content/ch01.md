## Outcome

LCS now has one vocabulary and one model of how its AI systems work, who is involved, and how systems move from idea to retirement.

## Section-by-section project results

| Section | Applied project | Completed result |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Scope | AI vocabulary boundary | Terminology applies to all LCS-developed, procured, and internally used AI systems. Conventional rules-only automation is recorded separately unless it has an AI component. |
| 2. References | Standards map | ISO/IEC 22989 is the vocabulary baseline; 42005 is used for system impacts; 42001 governs the organizational management system. |
| 3. Terms and definitions | Controlled glossary | The 14 terms below are mandatory in governance documents. |
| 4. Abbreviations | Acronym registry | AIMS, AIIA, RAG, ML, NLP, KPI, CAPA, and SoA are approved; undefined acronyms must be expanded at first use. |
| 5. AI concepts | Concept map | LCS recognizes Resolve and Atlas as NLP/RAG systems and Pulse as a supervised ML forecasting system. |
| 6. AI system life cycle | Lifecycle procedure | A nine-stage lifecycle with accountable owners and decision gates is adopted. |
| 7. Functional overview | Data-to-action trace | Each system now has documented inputs, model logic, outputs, action point, and human control. |
| 8. AI ecosystem | Dependency map | Providers, customers, data owners, cloud hosts, users, and affected people are mapped. |
| 9. Fields of AI | Use-case classification | Resolve and Atlas: NLP; Pulse: forecasting/data mining. No computer vision or autonomous action is in scope. |
| 10. Applications | Use-case cards | Three approved use-case cards define permissible purpose, users, prohibited uses, and review frequency. |
| Annex A | OECD lifecycle mapping | The LCS lifecycle is cross-referenced to conception, design/development, deployment, operation, and retirement. |

## Controlled glossary

| Term | LCS working definition | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| AI system | Engineered system producing outputs such as content, forecasts, recommendations, or decisions for human-defined objectives. | Defines the AIMS boundary. |
| AI provider | Entity providing an AI system or service. | LCS is a provider for Resolve; foundation-model vendors are providers to LCS. |
| AI customer | Entity acquiring or using an AI system. | LCS customers using Resolve must receive usage and limitation information. |
| AI subject | Person affected by an AI system’s output or use. | A customer asking support questions can be an AI subject. |
| Model | Logical or mathematical representation used to generate outputs. | Version and change control are required. |
| Training data | Data used to develop or tune a model. | Requires provenance and quality evidence. |
| Validation data | Data used to assess performance before release. | Supports release gates. |
| RAG | Pattern that retrieves approved information and gives it to a generative model as context. | Resolve and Atlas must cite their approved sources. |
| Intended use | Purpose, users, and conditions approved by LCS. | Sets the permitted operating boundary. |
| Foreseeable misuse | Predictable use outside intended use. | Included in impact and risk assessments. |
| Human oversight | A person can understand, challenge, stop, or override an AI-supported outcome. | Required for Resolve and critical Pulse decisions. |
| Trustworthiness | System qualities supporting justified trust, including reliability, robustness, transparency, fairness, safety, security, and privacy. | Becomes a control and monitoring theme. |
| Life cycle | Evolution from conception through retirement. | Ensures governance is not a one-time review. |
| Impact | Effect—beneficial or harmful—on individuals, groups, or society. | Assessed under ISO/IEC 42005. |

## AI concept and system classification

| System | AI field and approach | Inputs | Output | Constraint |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Resolve | NLP + RAG + generative model | Customer question, product/knowledge-base retrieval | Draft support response with citations | Agent approval required before transmission. |
| Atlas | NLP + RAG + generative model | Employee question, approved internal documents | Answer with source links and uncertainty notice | No HR, customer, or secrets-classified documents in its index. |
| Pulse | Supervised ML forecasting | Aggregated, pseudonymized product usage; historical demand | Forecast range and confidence band | May not be used for individual employee performance or staffing decisions without separate approval. |

## LCS AI lifecycle

| Stage | Owner | Required evidence / gate |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Inception | Product owner | Use-case card, business objective, initial risk triage. |
| 2. Design and development | Engineering lead | Architecture, data plan, model/vendor selection, security design. |
| 3. Verification and validation | QA and AI risk lead | Test results, bias/quality checks where relevant, red-team findings. |
| 4. Impact assessment | AI risk lead | Completed 42005 assessment and approval conditions. |
| 5. Deployment approval | AI governance board | Release decision, rollback plan, customer/employee communication. |
| 6. Operation and monitoring | Service owner | Performance, incidents, feedback, drift, and control operation records. |
| 7. Continuous validation | Engineering + QA | Scheduled quality and safety re-tests after model, data, or prompt changes. |
| 8. Re-evaluation | Governance board | Triggered reassessment after material change, incident, or new use case. |
| 9. Retirement | Product owner + security | Customer transition, data retention/deletion, access removal, final lessons learned. |

## Functional trace: Resolve

`Customer question → input filtering → approved-document retrieval → LLM drafting → citation/quality checks → agent review → customer response → monitoring and feedback`

Decision rights remain with the support agent. Resolve cannot send a response, issue refunds, change an account, or make eligibility decisions on its own.

## AI ecosystem and stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role | Need / expectation |
|---|---|---|
| LCS board and executives | Governing body | Responsible innovation, measurable risk control, legal compliance. |
| Product and engineering | Producer | Clear release gates, usable control requirements. |
| Foundation-model and cloud vendors | Suppliers | Contractual security, data-use, availability, and change commitments. |
| LCS support agents | Operators | Reliable drafts, source citations, ability to override. |
| Customers and customer end users | Customers / AI subjects | Accurate help, disclosure, privacy, clear human escalation. |
| Regulators and auditors | Relevant authorities | Evidence of accountability and controlled operation. |

## Use-case cards

| Use case | Approved | Prohibited | Review cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resolve | Draft support responses from approved documentation. | Autonomous sends; medical, legal, or financial advice; actions on accounts. | Monthly monitoring; reassess after material change. |
| Atlas | Retrieve approved internal technical and policy knowledge. | Indexing HR files, customer content, secrets-classified material, or making employment decisions. | Quarterly access/index review. |
| Pulse | Produce aggregated demand forecasts for planning. | Individual profiling or automated workforce decisions. | Quarterly accuracy and drift review. |
